Find your flow.

Highway Rush: weave through oncoming traffic. Near misses build your combo; your pace rises as your score grows. Nitro burns the blue bar. One hit ends the run.

Circuit modes: follow the green checkpoint posts in order. Finish races for credits and XP, tune your car in the garage, and race your own best ghost in Time Trial. Wet asphalt means less grip and longer braking, in every mode.

Gamepad: left stick steer, right trigger accelerate, left trigger brake, A boost/nitro, X handbrake, Y camera, Start pause.
